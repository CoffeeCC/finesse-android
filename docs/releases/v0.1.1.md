Finding your music in the car is quicker.

- **Search finds songs.** Results start with a **Top result**, then songs, artists, albums, playlists and audiobooks, closest match first. Your favourites and most-played come first when two match equally well. A song from search plays on through its album.
- **"Hey Google, play … on Finesse" works.** An artist plays shuffled, an album from the start, a song within its album, a playlist in order. "Play *song* by *artist*" works too. "Play music" shuffles everything.
- **What you play in the car counts.** It shows under Recently played, adds to play counts and shows as playing now, like Finesse in the browser.
- **Playlists play in your order**, not alphabetically.

**Update:** download `finesse-android-v0.1.1-libre-release.apk` below and open it. It installs over the app you have and you stay signed in.
